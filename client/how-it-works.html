Four steps. No shortcuts.

  1. Browse open problems — real problems from NGOs, companies, and government bodies, with full briefs.
  2. Submit your solution — draft with the 7-field framework, submit when ready.
  3. Get evaluated fairly — blind review on Clarity, Feasibility, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Potential, Implementation Depth.
  4. Build your Builder Score — cumulative, public, and shareable.

The 7-Field Submission Framework.

  1. Problem Understanding — restate the problem in your own words.
  2. Root Cause Analysis — go deeper than symptoms (5 Whys, systems thinking).
  3. Proposed Solution — specific mechanism, not just an idea.
  4. Feasibility Assessment — resources, timeframe, constraints.
  5. Expected Impact — who benefits, by how much, how measured.
  6. Risks & Limitations — what could go wrong, your assumptions.
  7. Implementation Plan — phases, dependencies, deployment.

Blind judging. Five criteria. No politics.

Judges see nothing about you — only your work. Each criterion rated 1–10.

  • Clarity of Thinking
  • Root Cause Depth
  • Solution Feasibility
  • Impact Potential
  • Implementation Depth
How scores become Builder Score

Average score × 10, adjusted for solutions completed. Consistency beats one exceptional submission.

Who can participate.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. SproutNet is about structured thinking across all domains.

Can I work in a team?

Team mode is available on problems where the poster enabled it.

How long do I have to submit?

Each problem has a deadline shown on its page. Save drafts and submit before it.

What happens after I submit?

Blind review; scores and feedback appear on your dashboard when judging completes.

Is this free?

Yes. Completely free for students. Always.
